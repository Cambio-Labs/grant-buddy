For nonprofit grant teams

Cited grant drafts, from your own documents.

Cambio Grant Buddy converts an RFP into a draft where every factual claim cites a source in your organization's documents. Claims without supporting evidence are flagged as gaps rather than filled in.

Every claim cites a source.

Each factual statement maps to a document in your organization. Where no document supports a claim, the tool reports a gap instead of writing an assertion.

Capabilities and limits.

It will

  • Cite every factual claim to your own documents
  • Show confidence: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, or a gap
  • Warn when your budget figure is over 12 months old
  • Check the funder's AI-use policy before drafting
  • Screen documents for names, dates of birth and case notes

It won't

  • Invent facts, numbers or partners
  • Submit anything to a funder
  • Use a participant story without documented consent
  • Write to your pipeline without showing and confirming the change
  • Keep your data inside the tool. It stays in your folder
Drafted with AI from your documents. A team member must review every claim before submission.

Bring your information, in any form.

On first use it helps you connect what you already have. When a connection is unavailable, it proceeds with documents you upload.

1

Connect

Google Drive, Notion, Sheets, Airtable, or a database. Read-only by default, verified with a non-mutating read.

2

Point to a folder

A synced or exported folder the tool can read. It records links, not copies.

3

Upload or paste

Documents and writing samples. Registered as pasted, with at most MEDIUM confidence.

Writing samples (LOI, full application, donor email, community) inform style only. They are never treated as evidence.

Set up in a few minutes.

Install the plugin, then run the setup command. A short guided session creates your organization's private pack.

/plugin marketplace add <github-org>/grant-assistant
/plugin install grant-assistant@cambio-labs
/grant-setup

Replace <github-org> with the account that hosts the repository.

Upload the plugin or the single-skill zip, then connect the folder that holds your org-pack/. Connect Google Drive and Notion from your connector settings if you use them.
